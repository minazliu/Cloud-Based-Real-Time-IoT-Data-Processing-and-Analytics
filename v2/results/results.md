# v2 results

Monte Carlo over **300 simulated 30-min recordings** (1 Hz, 12-bit ADC); each has a fire with p=0.5 and a steam/cooking nuisance event with p=0.5. Generated by `python -m v2.experiments`.

## E1  Filter accuracy (RMSE vs ground truth, ppm)

| filter | σ=20 | σ=40 | σ=60 | σ=80 | σ=120 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw (no filter) | 25.0 | 42.9 | 62.0 | 80.1 | 112.3 |
| Moving avg (N=8) | 10.3 | 16.2 | 22.6 | 29.1 | 40.1 |
| EMA (a=0.1) | 10.5 | 13.7 | 17.1 | 21.0 | 28.1 |
| Median5 + EMA | 11.3 | 14.7 | 18.0 | 22.2 | 30.7 |
| Kalman (gated, calibrated R) | 8.6 | 13.2 | 17.3 | 20.7 | 27.2 |

At the default noise (σ=40 ppm) the best filter is **Kalman (gated, calibrated R)**: 42.9 → 13.2 ppm (69% lower error).

## E3  Edge data reduction (smoke channel)

v1 streams every raw sample: 257 B/msg, 3600 msg/hr, cloud-side RMSE 43.0 ppm. Edge messages use a compact 72 B format, which alone accounts for 72% savings; the *messages/hr* column isolates the effect of filtering + send-on-delta.

| pipeline | deadband (ppm) | msgs / hr | bandwidth saved | RMSE (ppm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw + send-on-delta | 5 | 3348 | 73.9% | 43.0 |
| Raw + send-on-delta | 10 | 3103 | 75.8% | 43.0 |
| Raw + send-on-delta | 20 | 2613 | 79.7% | 43.0 |
| Raw + send-on-delta | 40 | 1739 | 86.5% | 43.0 |
| Raw + send-on-delta | 80 | 594 | 95.4% | 43.3 |
| Raw + send-on-delta | 160 | 91 | 99.3% | 47.9 |
| Kalman + send-on-delta | 5 | 1074 | 91.6% | 13.3 |
